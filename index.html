Who we are

Engineering clarity into every stage of construction.

KVK Construction & Engineering is being positioned as a Singapore-focused construction and engineering business. Our planned scope covers building construction, structural works, steel fabrication, reinforced-concrete works, installation and erection, project management and renovation.

Our intended operating approach centres on practical coordination, clear commercial documentation and a safety- and quality-minded delivery process. We help clients define responsibilities, schedules, changes and handover requirements before and during delivery.

  • Clear inclusions, exclusions and project assumptions
  • Coordinated teams, suppliers and project interfaces
  • Documented progress, variations and approvals
  • Responsible inspection and handover processes

How we work

A disciplined process from first enquiry to final handover.

Good delivery starts before mobilisation. We aim to make scope, cost, risk and responsibilities visible at every stage.

STEP 01

Enquiry & qualification

Understand the client, project scope, site, budget, timeline and payment terms.

STEP 02

Scope & site review

Review available drawings, BOQ, site information, interfaces and unknowns.

STEP 03

Costing & risk review

Assess labour, materials, equipment, quotations, risks and assumptions.

STEP 04

Contract & mobilisation

Confirm responsibilities, scope boundaries, schedule, insurance, permits, safety and quality requirements.

STEP 05

Execution & progress

Coordinate competent teams and suppliers; track progress, costs, risks, variations and decisions.

STEP 06

Inspection & handover

Complete applicable inspections, resolve defects, submit records and obtain client acceptance.
